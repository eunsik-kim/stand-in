Stand-In privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

Stand-In (stand-in.fly.dev) lets you rehearse conversations with AI characters. It was built by Eunsik Kim for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. This page says what the app collects, why, and who processes it.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in. When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account id, email address and name. We use them only to keep your stories under your account and to limit how much one account can use the service. We do not get your password or any other Google data.
  • What you write and say in a scene, the stories you create, and any photo you attach to one.
  • Your setup answers (the name to call you, how you usually talk, a few personality answers). They are kept in your browser and sent with requests so that suggested replies sound like you.
  • Camera, only if you turn it on. Gaze and head direction are computed in your browser. Video never leaves your device; only a few percentages (for example how much of the time you looked at the screen) are sent with each line.
  • Voice input, only if you use it. Speech recognition is done by your browser; in Chrome, Google's speech service processes the audio.
  • Your IP address, kept in server memory for an hour to limit requests per address.
  • How much your account uses each day (how many requests of each kind and an estimated cost), to enforce daily limits. The site's administrator can see this together with your account's email and name.

Who processes it

  • Meta Model API (Muse Spark and Muse Image) writes the scenes, the characters' lines and the photos from what you send.
  • OpenRouter and ElevenLabs turn the characters' lines into speech when voices are on.
  • Fly.io hosts the server (Tokyo region).

Storage

Stories you create, generated photos and voices, and a cache of model replies are stored on the server's disk. The app sets a sign-in cookie and keeps a random id and your settings in your browser's storage. There are no ads, no tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share your data for any other purpose.

Deleting your data

Email gracely9901@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete the stories and data stored under your account. You can remove Stand-In's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
